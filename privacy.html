Privacy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This page explains what CoreField's website collects when you sign up for early access. CoreField is made by LyfEscapes LLC.

What we collect

When you sign up, we collect the email address you enter and, if you choose to give them, your name, company and trade. We also record which page and ad link you came from.

How we use it

We use it only to email you about CoreField: when your trade's app is ready, how to start your free trial, and occasional product updates. We don't sell or share your information with anyone else for their marketing.

Who processes it

Sign-ups are delivered to us through a form service, which stores the submission so we can read it. Our website host may keep standard server logs.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to delete your information, by writing to support@lyfescapes.net.
